## Part II — Analyst app actions

Screens: [P1-05-User](./P1-05-user.md). Objects: [P1-02-User](./P1-02-user.md).

Analysts **read and act on** the intelligence feed — they do not configure clusters or queries. Cluster setup, scheduled runs, and article ingest are operator workflows ([P1-01-Admin](./P1-01-admin.md), [P1-06 Part I](./P1-06.md#part-i--admin-console-actions)). Ingested articles surface on Home → News and Signals.
